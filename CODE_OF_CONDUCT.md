# Code of conduct

QNode welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds and
experience levels. This policy applies to repository issues, discussions, pull requests,
and other project spaces.

Be respectful and address ideas rather than personal traits. Give technical criticism with
enough context to be useful. Do not harass, threaten, use discriminatory language, publish
someone else's private information, or repeatedly disrupt a discussion after being asked
to stop. Maintainers and contributors are expected to follow the same rules.

If you experience or witness a problem, email the maintainer privately at
[mhaque@edu.hse.ru](mailto:mhaque@edu.hse.ru) with a link, a description, and any context
you can safely share. Do not post personal details publicly. The maintainer will review
the report, hear from those involved as appropriate, and take proportionate action. That
may include a warning, editing or removing content, locking a discussion, or restricting
participation. The maintainer will avoid sharing report details beyond what is needed to
handle the issue, but cannot promise absolute confidentiality on third party platforms.

If a report concerns the maintainer, participants may use GitHub's platform reporting
mechanism. Security vulnerabilities should instead follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
